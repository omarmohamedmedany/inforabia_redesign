Our Services

Comprehensive, end-to-end IT solutions designed to empower your business. From seamless Oracle implementations to innovative cloud migrations, we provide the technical expertise and strategic vision needed to drive your digital transformation forward.

Oracle ERP Implementation & Migration

At Inforabia, we specialize in delivering robust Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP implementations tailored to your organization's unique operational requirements. Our extensive expertise spans across core Financial Management modules, ensuring seamless integration of General Ledger, Accounts Payable and Receivable, Fixed Assets, and Cash Management. By unifying your financial data, we empower your business with real-time insights, enhanced accuracy, and streamlined accounting processes that foster growth and compliance.

Beyond financials, our ERP solutions deeply integrate Procurement and Project Management capabilities. We optimize your supply chain with advanced Purchasing, Supplier Management, and Self-Service Procurement systems. Concurrently, our Project Management deployments offer precise Project Costing, intuitive Billing, and accurate Revenue Recognition. Paired with state-of-the-art Risk Management and Compliance frameworks, we ensure your enterprise remains secure, transparent, and poised for sustainable expansion.

Cloud Migration Services

Our Cloud Migration Services are designed to transition your legacy on-premise infrastructure into agile, scalable, and secure cloud environments. The journey begins with a comprehensive assessment of your existing systems, allowing us to craft a meticulous migration strategy and customized roadmap. Whether your destination is Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI), Amazon Web Services (AWS), or Microsoft Azure, our certified architects guide you through every phase of the transition with minimal disruption to your daily operations.

We employ a versatile range of migration methodologies—including lift-and-shift, re-platforming, and complete re-architecting—to suit your specific business goals. A critical component of our process is rigorous data migration and validation, ensuring zero data loss and maintaining absolute integrity. Post-migration, Inforabia provides continuous optimization, performance monitoring, and cost management, guaranteeing that your new cloud infrastructure operates at peak efficiency while delivering maximum return on investment.

Supply Chain Management (SCM)

Transform your supply chain into a resilient, responsive, and data-driven ecosystem with our Oracle SCM Cloud solutions. We expertly implement and optimize modules for Inventory Management, Manufacturing, and Order Management, enabling you to synchronize supply with demand seamlessly. By automating complex logistical processes and providing end-to-end visibility, we help you reduce operational costs, minimize delays, and dramatically improve delivery performance to your end customers.

We also integrate comprehensive Logistics and Transportation Management systems to streamline routing and freight operations. Human Capital Management (HCM)

Empower your workforce and streamline human resources operations with our customized Oracle HCM Cloud deployments. We lay a strong foundation with Core HR functionalities, seamlessly integrating Benefits and Compensation management to ensure employee satisfaction and retention. Our solutions are designed to automate administrative tasks, allowing your HR professionals to focus on strategic initiatives and fostering a positive, engaging workplace culture.

Our HCM offerings extend into sophisticated Talent Management, covering the entire employee lifecycle from Recruiting and Onboarding to Performance evaluation and Learning development. Drive strategic decision-making and operational excellence with our Oracle EPM Cloud implementations. Inforabia equips your financial teams with advanced tools for Financial Planning and Budgeting, enabling dynamic modeling, accurate forecasting, and agile resource allocation. Digital Transformation

Propel your business into the future with Inforabia's comprehensive Digital Transformation services. We integrate cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) technologies into your existing processes, unlocking predictive analytics and automating complex decision-making. By deploying Robotic Process Automation (RPA), we eliminate repetitive, manual tasks, significantly boosting operational efficiency and reducing human error across your organization.
