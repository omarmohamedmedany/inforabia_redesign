Common questions

Before we talk

How long does an Oracle implementation take?

Timing depends on scope, modules, data readiness, and change requirements. A focused finance implementation may take 3–6 months, while a full-suite deployment can run 9–18 months. We establish a realistic plan during discovery.

Which industries do you support?

Our experience includes telecommunications, healthcare, manufacturing, retail, finance, government, and other enterprise sectors across MENA.

Can you support remote or offshore delivery?

Yes. We offer on-site, remote, and blended delivery models, using clear collaboration practices to keep projects aligned and moving.

Can you migrate our on-premise Oracle environment to the cloud?

Yes. Cloud migration is a core capability, from assessment and planning through secure transition, validation, optimization, and support.

Do you provide post-implementation support?

Yes. Managed services can cover user support, monitoring, troubleshooting, updates, and continuous improvement after go-live.
