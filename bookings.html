Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical Oracle implementation take?

Implementation timelines vary greatly depending on the scope and modules chosen. A standard financial implementation might take 3-6 months, whereas a full suite deployment could span 9-18 months. We provide a realistic timeline during the custom proposal phase.

What industries do you specialize in?

We have deep expertise across various sectors including telecommunications, healthcare, manufacturing, and public sector. Our consultants are adept at customizing Oracle solutions to meet the specific regulatory and operational needs of these industries.

Do you offer remote/offshore support?

Yes, we provide both on-site and remote/offshore support options to suit your budget and preferences. Our robust remote collaboration tools ensure seamless communication and project execution, no matter where your team is located.

What is your pricing model?

We offer flexible pricing models including fixed-bid for well-defined projects, time-and-materials for dynamic requirements, and managed services retainers. We discuss these options transparently to find the best fit for your financial structure.

Can you migrate our existing on-premise Oracle to cloud?

Absolutely. Cloud migration is one of our core specialties. We ensure a secure, smooth transition with minimal downtime, modernizing your infrastructure to take full advantage of Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI).

Do you provide post-implementation support?

Yes, we offer comprehensive post-implementation managed services to ensure user adoption, system stability, and continuous improvement. Our team remains available for troubleshooting, updates, and further enhancements long after go-live.
